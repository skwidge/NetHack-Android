# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy covers the Android app with package name `com.skwidge.nethack` ("the app"), a free Android port of the game NetHack 3.6.7, distributed on Google Play.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Information the app stores on your device

The app stores these files on your device while you play:

- App preferences
- Save files
- Bones and level files
- The high-score list
- Game logs

This data stays on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it, except for any game logs saved to your device's shared Documents folder, which you can delete yourself.

## Network use

The app does not request internet access and does not send or receive data over the internet. An optional bones-sharing feature from earlier versions (Hearse) has been turned off.

## Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs and devices under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer receives only anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports through the Google Play Console.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
